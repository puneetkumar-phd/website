Professional summary

Computational biologist with a Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee and more than seven years of research experience at the intersection of molecular simulations, structure and ligand based drug design, and AI-driven discovery. Research interests include protein conformational dynamics, ligand-binding mechanisms, multi-omics analysis, and the prioritisation of small-molecule therapeutics.
