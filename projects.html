Computational workflow

From biological question to prioritised hypothesis

Projects are framed around mechanistic questions, integrated data, and clear validation pathways rather than isolated computational techniques.

1
DefineTranslate disease biology into a tractable molecular or systems-level question.
2
ModelCombine structural, dynamical, omics, and chemical data at the appropriate resolution.
3
PrioritiseRank mechanisms, targets, or compounds using interpretable evidence.
4
ValidateDesign computational checks and experimental collaborations that can falsify the hypothesis.
